Support

Last updated: 10.07.2026

If you have questions, need assistance, or want to report a problem related to Uniboard, you can contact us by email.

Email Support

Please send your support requests to:

Email: siteartstudio@gmail.com

When contacting us, please include:

  • A brief description of the issue
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • Uniboard app version
  • If the issue is related to the keyboard not appearing, please ensure you have enabled it in your iOS Settings (General > Keyboard > Keyboards).

Response Time

We aim to respond to support requests as soon as possible. Response times may vary depending on request volume.

Privacy Notice

Please do not send sensitive personal data, passwords, or confidential information by email.

Due to the privacy-first nature of Uniboard, we do not have access to your keystrokes, local typing history (bigrams), or any custom fonts and modifiers you have explicitly marked as "Private". Therefore, we cannot recover locally deleted private data.
